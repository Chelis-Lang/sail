## Why this measurement precedes another delivery change

The [actual failed server tail](m7-rpc-stage-workflow.md) selected complete
preencoded hits: frame selection p95 was 0.003 ms, without encoding. Its large
socket-output intervals include client backpressure. The
[complete-output control](m7-result-transfer-profile.md) showed that moving full
Python parsing after transfers reduced producer send p95 from 138.353 ms to
16.791 ms. Larger buffers reduced producer send wall time but barely changed
complete-client p95 and worsened its median. Those observations justify neither
another serialization cache nor a socket-buffer selection.

The original Python collector still receives and parses the entire graph. The
descriptor-bound product's actual freshness and complete-client costs have not
been sampled together. This scope will decide whether that substantive binding
change leaves a material delivery cost; offline components cannot answer that
live question. No report field, dependency, parse check or threshold is removed.
